OUR STORY

One team. Two things we're proud of.

Stucknot started as a way to prepare students for the industry while they were still in college. Along the way we started taking on real client work — and realized the two weren't separate: the best developers we trained were the ones who'd shipped something real, and the best client work came from people who'd been properly mentored, not just hired. So we stopped running them as two businesses.
